# HBL Savings Accounts

## HBL PLS Savings Account

### Overview
The HBL PLS (Profit and Loss Sharing) Savings Account is HBL's standard conventional savings
account, designed for customers who want to earn profit on their balance with no minimum balance
requirement and unlimited transactions.

### Minimum Balance
There is no minimum balance requirement to open or maintain a PLS Savings Account.

### Profit Rate and Payout
Profit is calculated on the monthly average balance and paid out on a semi-annual basis (every
six months). As of HBL's declared rates for January 2026, the savings account profit rate was
6.83% per annum, paid monthly. Profit rates are reviewed and republished by HBL roughly monthly
and vary with State Bank of Pakistan policy rate changes, so always confirm the current rate via
the customer care number or HBL's official rate sheet rather than treating this figure as fixed.

### Eligibility
Can be opened by both individuals and non-individuals/entities.

### Required Documents
- Computerized/Smart National Identity Card (CNIC/SNIC) for resident Pakistanis
- National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis (NICOP), or Pakistan Origin Card (POC) for
  overseas customers
- Proof/source of income documentation for individuals
- For non-individual/business accounts: identity documents of the business owner/directors plus
  income verification and standard supporting documents

### Fees
Unlimited transactions with no transaction charge. Other account-related charges follow HBL's
Schedule of Bank Charges (SOBC), which is separate from the profit-rate rate sheet.

## HBL Value Account

### Overview
The HBL Value Account is a higher-return savings account for customers willing to maintain a
higher average balance in exchange for a better profit rate than the standard PLS account.

### Minimum Balance
A monthly average balance of PKR 40,000 is required; falling below this triggers service charges
as per the prevailing Schedule of Bank Charges.

### Profit Rate and Payout
Profit is paid quarterly. The rate is tiered by deposit amount and, like all HBL deposit
products, changes periodically with market conditions — confirm the current tier rates via HBL's
official rate sheet or customer care before quoting a specific number to a customer.

### Account Limits
Only one Value Account may be opened per valid CNIC. Two free over-the-counter cash withdrawals
are allowed per calendar month; each additional withdrawal in that month is charged per the SOBC.
Debit cards are issued to individual account holders only.
