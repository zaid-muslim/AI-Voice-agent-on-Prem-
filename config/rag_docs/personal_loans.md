# HBL Personal Loans

## HBL Personal Loan (Conventional)

### Overview
An unsecured personal loan — no collateral required — for salaried and pensioner customers,
usable for purposes like medical expenses, education, weddings, or home repairs.

### Loan Amount
Available from PKR 25,000 up to PKR 3,000,000, depending on eligibility and the bank's
assessment criteria.

### Interest Rate
The interest rate varies by customer category, up to a maximum of 35.99% per annum. The exact
rate offered depends on the applicant's profile and is confirmed at the time of application.

### Eligibility
- Salary must be credited to an HBL account
- Age between 21 and 61 years
- Minimum salary of PKR 35,000 per month
- Pensioners are separately eligible for loans up to PKR 1,000,000

### Repayment
Repayable in equal monthly installments over a term of 12, 24, 36, 48, or 60 months, chosen by
the applicant.

### Documents and Application
No documents are required if applying via the HBL Mobile App for an eligible existing customer.
Branch applications require standard documentation (identity and income proof); check with a
branch or HBL PhoneBanking for the current in-branch document checklist.

## HBL Islamic Personal Financing

HBL also offers a Shariah-compliant equivalent to the conventional personal loan, called HBL
Islamic Consumer/Personal Financing, through its Islamic banking channel. It is structured as
Shariah-compliant financing (e.g. Murabaha or a similar Islamic financing structure) rather than
an interest-based loan — do not describe it using conventional loan/interest terminology.
