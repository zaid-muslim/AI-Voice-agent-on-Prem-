# HBL Digital Banking

## HBL Mobile App

### Overview
HBL's mobile banking app for iOS and Android, giving customers self-service access to most
everyday banking needs without visiting a branch.

### Fund Transfers
Supports transfers between HBL accounts as well as to other banks via 1LINK and M-net member
bank networks.

### Other Features
- View and download account statements, view transaction history
- Bill payments: utility bills, mobile top-ups, broadband, education payments, e-Vouchers
- Zakat and donation payments
- Credit card bill payments
- Purchase movie, bus, and event tickets; order food online
- QR code payments
- Invest in HBL mutual funds and open term deposits
- Manage debit and credit card blocking/unblocking directly in the app
- Set up standing instructions for recurring bill payments and fund transfers
- Manage and adjust personal transaction limits within the app

### Transaction Limits
Transaction/transfer limits are account- and channel-specific and are managed by the customer
within the app's limit settings; there is no single universal limit that applies to every
customer. For a specific limit question, direct the customer to check the app's limit management
screen or contact customer care.

## HBL Internet Banking

Provides similar self-service functionality to the mobile app (transfers, bill payments, account
statements) via a web browser, for customers who prefer banking from a desktop.

## HBL PhoneBanking

Automated and agent-assisted phone banking is available on 111-111-425 (UAN), including reporting
a lost or stolen card, general account queries, and complaint registration.
