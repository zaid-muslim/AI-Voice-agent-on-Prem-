# HBL Frequently Asked Questions

## Lost or Stolen Card

### What should I do if I lose my card?
Report it immediately to HBL PhoneBanking on 111-111-425. Once reported, the account is
immediately secured against unauthorized transactions on that card.

## Registering a Complaint

### How do I file a complaint?
There are multiple channels:
- Text "comp" followed by your CNIC to 4250 — a customer service representative will call the
  registered mobile number within 24 working hours.
- Email customer.complaints@hbl.com for account and payment-related issues.
- Use the online complaint registration form on HBL's website.
- Call HBL PhoneBanking on 111-111-425.

### What happens after I file a complaint?
The bank issues a complaint reference number via SMS, email, letter, or call within 2 working
days of the complaint being received. The Complaint Unit then works to resolve it, with an update
provided within 7 to 15 working days depending on the complexity of the issue.

### What if my complaint isn't resolved to my satisfaction?
Complaints can be escalated through HBL's Complaint Escalation Matrix. The Complaint Resolution
Unit is based at HBL's Head Office (9th Floor, Annexe Building, I.I. Chundrigar Road, Karachi)
and is overseen by the Senior Manager, Customer Resolutions.

## Account Opening

### What identity documents do I need to open an account?
Standard requirement is a valid CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card) or SNIC (Smart
National Identity Card) for resident Pakistanis. Overseas Pakistanis can use a NICOP (National
Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis) or a POC (Pakistan Origin Card). Specific accounts may
require additional documents such as proof of income — see the relevant account's own document in
this knowledge base (`savings_accounts.md`, `current_accounts.md`) for account-specific
requirements.
