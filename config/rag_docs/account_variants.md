# HBL Specialized Account Variants

## HBL Rutba Account (Senior Citizens)

HBL Rutba is a conventional (not Islamic/Shariah-compliant) savings account designed specifically
for senior citizens aged 55 and above, offering higher profit rates and monthly payouts in
exchange for no minimum balance requirement. It sits alongside HBL's standard savings accounts —
a customer who qualifies doesn't have to choose it, and eligibility (age 55+) should be confirmed
with the caller rather than assumed.

### Eligibility
Individuals aged 55 and above.

### Features
- No minimum balance requirement
- Profit is paid monthly (more frequent than the standard PLS savings account's semi-annual
  payout)
- Free first cheque book (10 leaves)
- Free personal accidental insurance and over-the-counter cash withdrawal insurance

### Positioning
Marketed as offering higher profit rates than the standard savings account, aimed at giving
senior citizens a stress-free, financially independent retirement. Confirm the current specific
rate via the official rate sheet, as it changes periodically like all HBL deposit products.

## HBL Nisa Accounts (Women-Focused)

HBL Nisa is a family of conventional (not Islamic/Shariah-compliant) accounts designed for women.
There are three Nisa account types, each a different account category: HBL Nisa Savings Account
(a savings account), HBL Nisa Asaan Account (a simplified-opening current account for new-to-bank
women), and HBL Nisa Plus Current Account (a full current account, including for female-owned
sole-proprietor businesses). A caller should be asked which one they mean, or told about all
three, rather than assuming there is only one Nisa account. Eligibility (being a woman) should be
confirmed with the caller rather than assumed.

### HBL Nisa Savings Account
Designed for women's saving needs: daily profit calculation, monthly profit payout, and free
women-centric insurance coverage for the first year (accidental death of spouse, permanent
accidental disability, and breast cancer hospitalization cover). Includes 50% off Safe Deposit
Locker fees for the first year.

### HBL Nisa Asaan Account
For new-to-bank female customers aged 18 and above who do not already have an HBL account. No
account maintenance charges. Specifically aimed at low-income, unbanked or underbanked women who
may lack a full documentary trail or proof of income — designed with simplified opening
requirements for that reason.

### HBL Nisa Plus Current Account
A current account for women, available to individuals and to female-owned sole-proprietor
businesses. Free issuance of HBL Classic (Mastercard) Debit Card and HBL Gold Debit Card for the
first year.
