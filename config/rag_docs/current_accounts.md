# HBL Current Accounts

## HBL Conventional Current Account

### Overview
A standard current account for customers who need unlimited transactions and do not require
profit payments. Suited to customers with frequent day-to-day banking activity.

### Features
Unlimited transactions with no profit paid on the balance (this is standard for current accounts,
not specific to HBL).

## HBL Basic Banking Account

### Overview
A simple account designed for customers with minimal transactional needs, meeting basic banking
requirements without the overhead of a full-featured current account.

### Withdrawal Limits
Unlimited free ATM withdrawals from HBL's own ATM network are allowed, though an ATM card
issuance/renewal fee is recovered annually per the Schedule of Bank Charges (SOBC). Up to two
free over-the-counter withdrawals per month are permitted; each additional over-the-counter
withdrawal is charged per the SOBC.

### Access Channels
Available through HBL Debit Card, Internet Banking, and HBL Phone Banking, 24 hours a day, 7 days
a week.

## HBL Asaan Account

### Overview
A current account designed for ease of opening, aimed at "New to Bank" (NTB) individuals — those
who do not already have an HBL account.

### Eligibility
Individuals aged 18 and above. Can be opened using a CNIC or SNIC (Smart National Identity Card).

### Features
- Inward remittance is allowed with no limit; outward and commercial remittance is restricted.
- Free PAYPAK debit card for the first year; charges apply from the second year onward per the
  prevailing SOBC.
- As a current account, no profit is paid on the balance.
- Up to four free over-the-counter debit withdrawals per calendar month; charges apply beyond
  that, per the SOBC.
