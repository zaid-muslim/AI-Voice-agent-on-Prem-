# HBL Home Remittance Services

## Overview

HBL's Home Remittance service lets Pakistanis living abroad send money home instantly,
conveniently, and safely through legal banking channels, with multiple ways to transfer funds.

## HBL FastTransfer

### Overview
HBL FastTransfer allows sending remittances from anywhere in the world back to Pakistan quickly
and, for the sender, free of charge — HBL does not charge the sender a fee for this service.
Senders receive a confirmation SMS once the money has been collected by the beneficiary.

### Receiving Options
- Beneficiaries can collect the sent money in cash from any of HBL's 1,600+ branches across
  Pakistan, without needing to have an HBL account themselves.
- Alternatively, the remittance can be deposited directly into any HBL account, or into an
  account at another Pakistani bank.

### Tracking
The HBL Remittance Tracker lets senders check the real-time status of a transfer by entering the
transaction's reference number.

## Konnect Home Remittance Account

A dedicated account product tailored for use in receiving/managing home remittance funds — ask a
representative for current details if a caller asks specifically about this product, since it has
its own eligibility and feature set separate from the standard remittance service.
