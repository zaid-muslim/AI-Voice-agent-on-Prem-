# HBL Credit Cards

## Card Types

HBL offers several credit card tiers: HBL Platinum CreditCard, HBL Gold CreditCard, HBL Green
CreditCard, and HBL FuelSaver CreditCard (available in Gold and Green variants).

### HBL Platinum CreditCard
A premium card offering Visa privileges, airport lounge access, and lifestyle/travel benefits
alongside standard rewards.

### HBL Gold and Green CreditCard
Mid-tier cards offering reward points on spending, exclusive shopping and dining offers, and
dedicated phone banking service, with the flexibility to pay later for purchases.

### HBL FuelSaver CreditCard
Offers cashback on fuel transactions — up to 5% cashback on fuel purchases, capped so a
cardholder can save up to roughly PKR 2,000 per month.

## Annual Fees

Annual fees vary by card tier, roughly PKR 1,500 to PKR 12,000 depending on the card:
- HBL Mastercard World: PKR 12,000 (can be waived on high annual spend)
- HBL Platinum Visa: fee reversal available if spend reaches PKR 500,000 within 60 days of the
  statement generation date
- HBL Gold: fee reversal available if spend reaches PKR 200,000 within 60 days
- HBL Green: fee reversal available if spend reaches PKR 50,000 within 60 days

Exact current annual fees and waiver thresholds should be confirmed against HBL's published Key
Fact Sheet for the specific card, since these are periodically revised.

## Markup Rate

The markup (interest) rate on outstanding credit card balances is approximately 3.5% per month,
subject to State Bank of Pakistan regulations and periodic revision.

## Rewards Program

Activating an HBL Credit Card automatically enrolls the cardholder in the HBL Rewards Program at
no extra fee.
- Points-based rewards: earn HBL Rewards points on every purchase, redeemable for cashback,
  vouchers, or merchandise.
- HBL Platinum Visa Rewards: 1 point per PKR 25 spent locally, 3 points per PKR 25 spent on
  foreign transactions.
- Additional benefits include discounts at partner restaurants, hotels, retail stores, and online
  platforms across Pakistan.

## Lost or Stolen Card

Report a lost or stolen card immediately to HBL PhoneBanking on 111-111-425 to secure the account
against unauthorized transactions. See `faq.md` for the full reporting and complaint process.
